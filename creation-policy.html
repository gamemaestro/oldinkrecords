How We Make Things

Creation Policy

We believe in transparency. This is an overview of how things are made at Old Ink Records. Honesty about our process is important to us.

Effective November 2026. Last updated November 2026.

Works credited to Old Ink Records

Works credited to Old Ink Records are made by humans working with tools, including AI writing, image, video, motion, modeling, and graphics tools. A human conceives every work, directs it, reviews it, and approves it before it is published. Nothing leaves Old Ink Records without being imagined and shaped by a human, even when assisted with AI. Not every Old Ink Records work uses AI tools, and each one states how it was made.

Works under Jorden Lacy Jackson’s name

Every word and image in publications and productions that carry Jorden Lacy Jackson’s name as author is created by Jorden. No AI tool, editor, co-writer, or ghostwriter writes or draws any part of these works. Jorden may use AI tools as a thinking partner for research, planning, and worldbuilding structure. The story, the voice, every word, and every image are hers. This protects the full integrity of the author’s imagination: these works travel directly from her to you, imperfections included.

Disclosure

Every publication and product page says how the work was made. When AI tools were used, we say so plainly.

What we never do

  • Let AI tools write or draw any part of a work published under a person’s name.
  • Present AI-generated art as hand-made.
  • Prompt AI tools to imitate a living artist by name.
  • Write reviews, testimonials, or endorsements in invented voices.

Research and sources

We check facts against their sources and list those sources in the work.

Changes

Tools change, and so will this policy. When it does, we update this page and note the date.
