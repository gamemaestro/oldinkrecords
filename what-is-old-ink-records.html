A Solarpunk Scriptorium

Solarpunk Is an Inside Job

At Old Ink Records we believe every person has a story worth telling, a life worth documenting, a cosmology worth building, and that this work directly contributes to the greater good. We reject the idea that art and creativity are the province of celebrities and credentialed professionals. They are the birthright of every human being.

This is why Old Ink Records is a Solarpunk Scriptorium: we are building towards a future where every human has the safety and support to self-actualize and serve the collective with their gifts, where humans live harmoniously with nature, technology, and each other, and where the imagination is understood as the most powerful transformative tool available to us.

The Record Keepers

Looking Back

At Old Ink Records, we believe worldbuilding and record keeping are sacred human acts. Every record keeps what was; every world we imagine keeps a record of what could be. From the first female scribes of ancient Mesopotamia to the nuns who preserved civilization in medieval scriptoria, the keepers of records have always done some of humanity's most important work.

Old Ink Records exists to honor that lineage and continue it through art, technology, original research, published histories, tools and resources for anyone curious about their own histories, mysteries, and records.

Looking Forward
