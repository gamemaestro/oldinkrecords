Old Ink Records

Research, Record Keeping and Legacy Work

At Old Ink Records, we believe record keeping is a sacred human act. From the first female scribes of ancient Mesopotamia to the nuns who preserved civilization in medieval scriptoria, the keepers of records have always done some of humanity's most important work.

Old Ink Records exists to honor that lineage and continue it through original research, published histories, metaphysical inquiry, tools and resources for anyone curious about their own histories, mysteries, and records.
