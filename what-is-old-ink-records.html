Old Ink Records

A Solarpunk Research and Record Keeping Institution

At Old Ink Records, we believe record keeping is a sacred human act. From the first female scribes of ancient Mesopotamia to the nuns who preserved civilization in medieval scriptoria, the keepers of records have always done some of humanity's most important work.

Old Ink Records exists to honor that lineage and continue it through art, technology, original research, published histories, metaphysical inquiry, tools and resources for anyone curious about their own histories, mysteries, and records.

A Solarpunk Scriptorium

Solarpunk Is an Inside Job

Old Ink Records was founded on the premise that the most radical and generative act available to a human being is the sacred work of individuation. Discovering who God made you to be, developing that fully, and offering it to the world is a duty and an act of devotion. Old Ink Records is taking the solarpunk thesis out of the ether and making it personal.

The world gets better through individuals who have done the inner work of becoming fully themselves, then show up as that, completely, in every space they occupy.

At Old Ink Records we believe every person has a story worth telling, a life worth documenting, a cosmology worth building, and that this work directly contributes to the greater good. We reject the idea that art and creativity are the province of celebrities and credentialed professionals. They are the birthright of every human being.

This is why Old Ink Records is a Solarpunk Scriptorium: we are building toward the same future, one in which humans live harmoniously with nature, technology, and each other, and the imagination is understood as the most powerful transformative tool available to us.

Founded on August 26, 2026 in Los Angeles by Jorden Lacy Jackson.
