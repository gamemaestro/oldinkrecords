Old Ink Records Grounds Its Research and Record Keeping in Solarpunk, Becoming an Experiential Engine for the Movement

The research and record keeping institution launches publicly online with a Solarpunk Scriptorium, a Solarpunk Sandbox, and a home on YouTube, carrying forward a vision born in California and shared around the world

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

LOS ANGELES, CA, November 19, 2026: Old Ink Records, the research and record keeping institution founded in Los Angeles on August 26, 2026, today grounds its work in the solarpunk movement and launches publicly online. Its aim is to serve as an engine for solarpunk: a place that keeps the movement's record, studies its roots, and gives people the stories, research, and practices to help build the future it describes.

Solarpunk imagines a sustainable, just, and beautiful future and asks people to start building it now. Its vision was born in California, in the work of Octavia E. Butler in Pasadena, Ursula K. Le Guin, born in Berkeley, and Kim Stanley Robinson, who made his home in Davis. Its first stories came from Brazil, and today it is a global movement of writers, artists, technologists, gardeners, and builders. Old Ink Records adds to this work from Los Angeles.

“We started as a research and record keeping institution, drawing inspiration for the solarpunk movement. Eventually we realized it made sense to ground ourselves in the movement and expand on it,” said Jorden Lacy Jackson, founder of Old Ink Records.

That work moves in two directions at once. The Solarpunk Scriptorium looks back, publishing Histories, investigating Mysteries, and keeping the record of our own lives. The Solarpunk Sandbox looks forward, dreaming and experimenting with the world that is coming through videos, stories, and worlds you can step into.

Old Ink Records' public launch begins on the Old Ink Records website (oldinkrecords.com) and YouTube channel (youtube.com/@oldinkrecords) where the institution's research and records will be shared. In addition to these, the experimental work is done on the founder's channel, Jorden Lacy Jackson · Solarpunk Sandbox (youtube.com/@jordenlacyjackson), exploring what solarpunk is, where it came from, and how to make it part of your mindset and lifestyle. Coming soon is the Old Ink Records podcast.

To mark the public launch, Old Ink Records has released its first publication, Solarpunk: A History of the Movement, an illustrated e-book that traces solarpunk from Octavia E. Butler's California to the global movement of today. It is available now for $7 at oldinkrecords.com.

About Old Ink Records

Old Ink Records is a solarpunk research and record keeping institution founded in Los Angeles in 2026 by Jorden Lacy Jackson. Its guiding belief is that solarpunk is an inside job: the world gets better through people who do the inner work of becoming fully themselves and show up as that in every space they occupy. Old Ink Records believes record keeping is a sacred human act, that every person has a story worth telling and a life worth documenting, and that art and creativity are the birthright of every human being. It continues the lineage of the record keepers through art, technology, original research, and published histories. oldinkrecords.com
