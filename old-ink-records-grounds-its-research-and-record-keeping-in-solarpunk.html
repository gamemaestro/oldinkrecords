Old Ink Records Grounds Its Research and Record Keeping in Solarpunk Worldbuilding, Becoming an Engine for the Movement

The research and record keeping institution officially launches to the public with its first publication. Its worlds include a Solarpunk Scriptorium, a Solarpunk Sandbox, and a home on YouTube, carrying forward a vision born in California and shared around the world

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

LOS ANGELES, CA, November 19, 2026: Old Ink Records, the research and record keeping institution founded in Los Angeles in 2026, today grounds its work in the solarpunk movement and launches publicly online, offering its first publication, Solarpunk: A History of the Movement. Its aim is to serve as an engine for solarpunk: a place that keeps the movement’s record, studies its roots, and gives people the inspiration, stories, research, worlds, and practices to help build the future the movement describes.

Solarpunk imagines a sustainable, just, and beautiful future and asks people to start building it now. Its vision was born in California, in the work of Octavia E. Butler in Pasadena, Ursula K. Le Guin, born in Berkeley, and Kim Stanley Robinson, who made his home in Davis. Its first stories came from Brazil, and today it is a global movement of artists, storytellers, naturalists, technologists, and builders. Old Ink Records adds to this work from Los Angeles.

“Old Ink Records started as a research and record keeping institution, drawing inspiration from the solarpunk movement. It quickly became clear that the way forward was to ground ourselves in the movement and expand on it,” said Jorden Lacy Jackson, founder of Old Ink Records.

That work moves in two directions at once. The Solarpunk Scriptorium reaches into the past, publishing Histories, investigating Mysteries, and keeping the record of our own lives. The Solarpunk Sandbox looks forward, dreaming, experimenting, and drafting what comes next through videos, stories, and worlds you can step into.

Old Ink Records’ public launch begins on the Old Ink Records website (oldinkrecords.com) and YouTube channel (youtube.com/@oldinkrecords), where the institution’s research and records will be shared. The experimental work happens on the founder’s channel, Jorden Lacy Jackson · Solarpunk Sandbox (youtube.com/@jordenlacyjackson), exploring what solarpunk is, where it came from, how it’s evolving today, and how to make it part of your mindset and lifestyle.

To mark the public launch, Old Ink Records has released its first publication, Solarpunk: A History of the Movement, an illustrated e-book that traces solarpunk from Octavia E. Butler’s California to the global movement of today. It is available now for $7 at oldinkrecords.com.

About Old Ink Records

Old Ink Records is a solarpunk research, record keeping, and worldbuilding institution founded in Los Angeles in 2026 by Jorden Lacy Jackson. Its guiding belief is that solarpunk is an inside job: the world gets better through people who do the inner work of becoming fully themselves and show up as that in every space they occupy. Old Ink Records believes that worldbuilding and record keeping are sacred human acts, that every person has a story worth telling and a life worth documenting, and that art and creativity are the birthright of every human being. It continues the lineage of the record keepers through art, technology, original research, publishing, and production.
