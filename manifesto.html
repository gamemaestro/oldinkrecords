The Old Ink Records Manifesto

We build blueprints and keep records of humanity’s histories, mysteries, comedies and potential futures.

  1. 1

    Innovation always starts as imagination.

    Every advancement and discovery begins as a record before it becomes tangible in the real world: a dream, a story, a sketch, a letter, a plan. We keep those records, and make new ones.

  2. 2

    Worldbuilding and record keeping are sacred human acts.

    Every record preserves what was. Every world we imagine inspires what could be.

  3. 3

    Solarpunk is an inside job.

    The world gets better through people who have done the work of becoming fully themselves. We build systems, tools, and blueprints to support that work.

  4. 4

    Self-actualization is a birthright.

    Art, story, and worldbuilding belong to every person, with or without “official” credentials.

  5. 5

    A human imagines every piece of our work.

    We use the best tools available to research, build, and share, including AI. A human conceives every work, directs it, and answers for it. Technology is used to help with execution, not ideation.

  6. 6

    We believe in transparency.

    We tell you how things are made. Honesty about our process is part of the record. Every publication says who made it and how it was created. Read our Creation Policy.

  7. 7

    We choose optimism.

    Dystopia is easy to imagine. We believe in fighting for positive outcomes, even when they don’t seem “realistic” in the present moment. We believe in miracles and choose to do the transformative work of imagining what can go right. Then we do what we can to build it and make it tangible.

Old Ink Records, Los Angeles, November 2026
