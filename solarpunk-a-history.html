Solarpunk: A History of the Movement

From Octavia Butler's California to a global movement

Dystopia has had the collective imagination for a long time. Solarpunk asks what a sustainable, just, and beautiful future actually looks like, and a growing movement is building the answer. This history follows solarpunk from Octavia E. Butler's California to the word's first appearance in 2008 and the global movement it has become: the writers who planted the seeds, the people shaping it now, the criticisms worth taking seriously, and why Old Ink Records makes it personal.
