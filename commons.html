How to use the Commons

Free for anything. Every image is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Use it in your posts, zines, slides, videos, books, or products, personal or commercial. Edit it, remix it, print it.

Credit appreciated, never required. If you’d like to share where it came from: Art: Old Ink Records Commons (oldinkrecords.com/commons)

How it was made. These images were made by Old Ink Records with AI image tools, and each one was chosen and reviewed by a person. Read our Creation Policy.

Where it came from. Every file carries its origin inside it: creator, credit line, license, and a link back to this page. Our provenance record lists each image with its first publication date and a digital fingerprint, so the original can always be traced home.

What isn’t included. The Old Ink Records name and Phi mark are trademarks and are not part of the Commons.
