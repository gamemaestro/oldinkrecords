Support

Help with your order

Email info@oldinkrecords.com and we'll reply within 2 business days.

To help us help you, include

The email address you used at checkout, your receipt or order number, and a short description of the problem. For a damaged or misprinted item, please attach photos.

Common questions

I didn't get my download. After payment you are taken straight to your download page, and Stripe emails your receipt. Check your spam folder for the receipt, then email us and we'll send the file.

My file won't open. Our e-books are PDFs and open in any browser or PDF reader. If the file still won't open, email us and we'll send a fresh copy.

Can I get a refund? All sales are final, with exceptions for damaged, defective, or undelivered orders. See our Terms.
