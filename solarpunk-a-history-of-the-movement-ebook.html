The future doesn’t have to look like a disaster movie. Solarpunk is the movement imagining a sustainable, just, and beautiful future, and asking people to start building it now. This is its story: where it came from, who shaped it, what it believes, and where it’s headed.

Why read it now

Hope Has a History

Dystopia has held the collective imagination for a long time. Solarpunk offers another way to see what’s coming, and its roots run deeper than most people realize: through the novels of Octavia E. Butler and Ursula K. Le Guin, an anonymous blog post in 2008, the first anthology published in Brazil, and the artists, technologists, and builders carrying it forward today.

Whether you’re meeting solarpunk for the first time or you’ve been dreaming in it for years, this history gives you the whole story in one sitting, with sources for everything.
