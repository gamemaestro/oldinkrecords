Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Old Ink Records is operated by Jacks Method, LLC in Los Angeles, California. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit oldinkrecords.com, buy from us, or contact us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you buy something: your name, email address, billing details, and, for physical products, your shipping address. Payments are handled by Stripe. Old Ink Records never sees or stores your full card number.

When you email us: your email address and whatever you include in your message.

When you visit the site: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not build a profile of you. Our host, Cloudflare, also processes basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

How we use it

We use your information to deliver your purchase, send receipts and download links, ship physical orders, answer your messages, prevent fraud, and keep the records the law requires for taxes and accounting. We send marketing emails only if you choose to sign up, and every one includes a way to unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run the shop: Stripe for payments, Cloudflare for hosting and analytics, Google Workspace for email, and, for physical products, the printing and shipping partners that make and deliver your order. Each handles your information under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, generally up to seven years. We keep emails only as long as they are useful for helping you.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we are required to keep it. Email info@oldinkrecords.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Do Not Track: because we do not track visitors across websites, our site works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Children

Our site and products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

We rely on established providers with strong security practices, and we limit access to your information to what is needed to serve you. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care with what you trust us with.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy: info@oldinkrecords.com
